I visited Prof. Sung Kyun Hong's GNC Lab at **Sejong University** and gave a seminar on **fault diagnosis and fault-tolerant control for aerospace systems**.

Few research groups in Korea focus on this area, so it was valuable to meet researchers working on similar problems. The discussion after the talk covered data-driven fault diagnosis, fault-tolerant flight control, and verification strategies for safety-critical autonomous systems. I also toured the lab's hardware-in-the-loop simulation environment and satellite test facilities, a good reminder of how important experimental verification is for moving algorithms onto real hardware.

Thanks to Prof. Hong and the lab members for the warm welcome and the discussion.
