Professional Summary

GNC researcher working on AI-based guidance and control, fault diagnosis and fault-tolerant control for eVTOL and multirotor platforms, and GNSS-denied navigation with quantum magnetometers. Ph.D. dissertation received the KAIST College of Engineering Outstanding Ph.D. Dissertation Award (2026).

Experience

    Postdoctoral Researcher
    KAIST — AITA InnoCORE Program, Dept. of Aerospace Engineering
    Lead researcher on AI-based fault-tolerant control and quantum-magnetometer-aided navigation (MagNav) for eVTOL and unmanned aerial systems (advisor: Prof. Hyochoong Bang).
    • Mentor graduate and undergraduate students on disturbance-observer design, data-driven system identification, and drone-based aeromagnetic surveys.
    • Coordinate flight-test campaigns integrating VN-200 INS, ZED-F9P RTK-GPS, and magnetometer payloads for GNSS-denied navigation.
